# Brand identity

The approved songsmith identity pairs a paper songbird on the left with a sculpted lowercase wordmark on the right. The folded wing connects song, illustration, and craft. An asymmetric silhouette gives the emblem its own identity without combining literal music-note and filmstrip symbols.

## Approved asset

Use songsmith-logo.png as the current horizontal lockup. The user approved this design on September 7, 2026. Preserve the bird, lettering, relative proportions, indigo-and-apricot palette, and transparent background. Changes to the concept require a new user request.

The original PNG is 2172 × 724 pixels with alpha transparency. ImageGen was asked for the highest available native resolution, preferably 3840 × 1280, but returned this size. It has not been upscaled and must not be described as native 4K. It is raster artwork, not an editable vector master.

## Placement

Display the complete lockup at 480 × 160 pixels at the top of the README. Keep it centered with the project description and navigation below, followed by the film preview. Preserve the aspect ratio and allow it to shrink with the container. Do not split the name below the emblem, add an enclosing tile, or add shadows and decorative badges.

The approved horizontal composition is the primary signature. A future standalone icon should retain this exact bird rather than introduce another symbol.

## Design method

Frontend-design guided the project-specific concept, the relationship between emblem and lettering, and the separation between brand header and film demonstration. UI/UX Pro Max was consulted for layout hierarchy, image scaling, alternative text, and asset weight. Its generic website templates were not adopted. The native PNG is approximately 399 KB.

[The Wildest](https://www.dandad.org/work/d-ad-awards-archive/the-wildest-brand-identity) informed the value of a distinctive typographic gesture. [MIT Media Lab](https://www.dandad.org/work/d-ad-awards-archive/mit-media-lab-identity) informed coherent construction. [Gretel's IFC identity](https://gretelny.com/ifc) informed a brand system that leaves room for its content. These are conceptual references, not copied artwork or claimed affiliations.

## Generation

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool, without a supplied reference image. The exact generation prompt follows; it records intent rather than guaranteeing exact generated geometry or pixel colors.

Use case: logo-brand.
Produce the highest available native resolution final brand identity asset. Request a 3840 x 1280 pixel wide horizontal transparent PNG if supported; prioritize pristine large-resolution edges and exact typography, do not simulate resolution with mockups.
Brand: "songsmith", a creative code workshop turning lyrics and music into finely illustrated animated films.
REQUIRED LAYOUT: a distinct standalone emblem on the LEFT, the full wordmark "songsmith" on the RIGHT, aligned as one horizontal signature. Both elements are mandatory.
Original concept: a "paper songbird". A single elegant asymmetric swift-like bird formed from three decisive folded-paper planes, sweeping gently upward toward the right. Its broad raised wing evokes a lyric page becoming alive; a small split tail suggests two short lyric lines. Convey flight and making, not an airline seal. Strong compact angular silhouette, subtle rounded joins, clever but very simple flat folds. No circular enclosure, no central hole, no eyes, no face, no human forms. The bird should be recognizable alone at 32px. No musical-note symbol, filmstrip, brackets, quote marks, play-button triangle, flowers, or intertwined shapes. Avoid complex origami, line-art details, feather outlines, corporate eagle heraldry, and resemblance to existing brands.
Wordmark exactly "songsmith", lowercase s-o-n-g-s-m-i-t-h. Bespoke upright warm contemporary lettering with softly flared terminals and medium-bold substantial strokes. Carefully shaped s, rhythmic m/h arches, a legible single-storey g with a compact open descender, custom but effortless readability. The typography should feel like an independent creative studio, not a generic tech font, antique newspaper masthead, cursive script, or cartoon. Optical kerning, no touching letters, no distortion.
Color system: iris indigo #6858D6 for the wordmark and most of the bird, with one small apricot #EAA76B folded plane in the bird. Only two flat solid colors, clear edges. Genuinely transparent alpha background.
Composition: bird occupies about 17% of the logo's total width, gap about half the bird's width, wordmark about 73%. Bird visually slightly taller than wordmark ascenders. Plenty of clean internal space. Center the lockup, filling about 88% of the canvas width, leave even modest outer margins. Keep the complete bird and every letter visible.
Build an original coherent silhouette and letterforms from a disciplined common shape language. Borrow the principles of distinctive typographic gesture, optical balance, and scalable identity systems seen in acclaimed identity design, never copy an award-winning logo.
Output ONE final logo only. No presentation board, no mockup, no explanatory labels, no tagline, no award logos, no watermarks, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no bevel, no 3D, no glow, no app tile.
